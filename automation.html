Speed to lead

Hot leads get a call task in minutes and broker alerts at 1 and 4 hours if nobody has called.

Right lead, right path

Leads are scored hot, warm or cold on intake; leads below the threshold are guarded out of the sales ladder and nurtured instead.

Clear ownership

Every lead, reply and booking has an owner, a backup and an escalation timer, so nothing sits unanswered.

Compliant by design

Consent records, quiet hours, STOP and unsubscribe handling, and credit licence wording are built into the flows.
