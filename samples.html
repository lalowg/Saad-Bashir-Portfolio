Excel

Job Application Tracker

A five-sheet workbook for running a job search on someone's behalf: an Applications table with dropdowns and validation, a Companies lookup sheet, list tables that feed the dropdowns, a dashboard that is 100 percent formulas, and a formula guide explaining every calculation.

Download the workbook (.xlsx) View the formula guide
  • XLOOKUP with an INDEX/MATCH fallback, VLOOKUP, INDEX/MATCH and TRIM for clean lookups
  • COUNTIFS, SUMIFS, AVERAGEIFS, SUMIF and COUNTIF with array constants
  • Date formulas with TODAY, EOMONTH, WEEKDAY and TEXT for weekly and monthly reporting
  • Nested IF follow-up flags: Overdue, Due today, Scheduled, Closed
  • Data validation dropdowns, nine conditional-formatting rules, a reconciliation check and a chart
